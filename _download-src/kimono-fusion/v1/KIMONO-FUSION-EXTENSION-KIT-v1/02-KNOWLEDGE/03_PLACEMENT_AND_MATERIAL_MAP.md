# PLACEMENT & MATERIAL MAP

## Purpose
Defines where visual elements belong and what production method each zone should imply.

## Zone map

### Z01 — INNER_BACK_HERO
Priority: MAXIMUM
Coverage: usually 55–85% of usable inner jacket area.
Use for:
- main artwork;
- main character;
- main landscape;
- primary abstract composition.

Material behavior:
- high-definition printed / sublimated lining or integrated inner panel;
- visible textile grain;
- natural fold distortion;
- seams remain visible;
- artwork follows the garment, not a perfect digital rectangle.

### Z02 — INNER_LEFT_PANEL
Priority: HIGH
Use for:
- continuation of hero artwork;
- secondary motifs;
- atmospheric extension.

Do not place an unrelated second artwork here.

### Z03 — INNER_RIGHT_PANEL
Same rules as Z02.

### Z04 — UPPER_BACK_EMBLEM
Priority: MEDIUM
Default size: restrained, approximately hand-sized in product photography.
Production:
- embroidery or woven patch.
Content:
- simplified emblem derived from real source-art geometry.

### Z05 — LEFT_SHOULDER_OR_SLEEVE
Priority: MEDIUM
Production:
- embroidered or woven circular/crest patch;
- occasional direct embroidery.

Keep smaller than the upper-back emblem unless the user asks otherwise.

### Z06 — FRONT_LOWER_HEM_LABEL
Priority: LOW
Production:
- small woven brand-style label;
- source-art micro motif;
- no large hero graphics.

### Z07 — REAR_LOWER_HEM_LABEL
Priority: LOW
Optional.
Must remain consistent in every view.

### Z08 — PANTS_MICRO_MARK
Priority: LOW
Optional tiny embroidered or woven motif near lower leg or hip.
Never compete with the jacket.

### Z09 — BELT_ACCENT
Keep the belt structurally traditional.
If a red rank bar / end detail is present, preserve it consistently.
Do not recolor the whole belt based on the artwork unless explicitly requested.

## Exterior restraint rule
Default maximum exterior graphic hierarchy:
- 1 upper-back emblem;
- 1 shoulder/sleeve emblem;
- 1–2 tiny labels;
- optional tiny pants mark.

The hero illustration should not be repeated in full on the exterior.

## Art crop logic
When adapting hero artwork:
1. keep the primary anchor unobstructed;
2. place face / main symbol away from major lapel overlap when possible;
3. let secondary energy / pattern flow into side panels;
4. use seams as natural visual breaks;
5. avoid cutting key facial features at panel edges;
6. preserve recognizability before filling every centimeter.

## Material realism by application

### Embroidery
Use for:
- emblems;
- line marks;
- compact symbols.
Visual cues:
- thread relief;
- satin stitch or dense fill;
- subtle edge thickness;
- no glossy sticker edges.

### Woven patch
Use for:
- compact multi-line symbols;
- small labels;
- intricate crest shapes.
Visual cues:
- woven border;
- stitched edge;
- textile integration.

### Printed / sublimated interior
Use for:
- full-color hero art;
- gradients;
- painterly effects;
- complex scenes;
- photographic art.
Visual cues:
- art remains in textile surface;
- no decal shine;
- no floating layer;
- texture remains visible through print.

## Front exterior
Keep visually clean.
The user should experience the interior as a reveal.

## Back exterior
One emblem should carry the identity of the art without exposing the full hero composition.

## Consistency rule
Any placement defined once becomes locked for all subsequent views of the same MASTER PRODUCT.
