# MANUFACTURING REALISM

## Goal
Make the design look manufacturable and physically integrated.

## Exterior jacket
Preferred visual cues:
- dense woven cotton texture;
- visible individual weave pattern at close range;
- thick lapel;
- reinforced stitching;
- slight natural garment wrinkles;
- weight and drape consistent with martial-arts apparel.

Avoid:
- smooth neoprene look;
- vinyl;
- glossy plastic;
- puffer insulation;
- fashion-shell nylon as main exterior.

## Pants
Keep functionally plausible:
- matching base color;
- straight legs;
- reinforced knees;
- durable weave;
- minimal graphics.

## Interior hero application
For complex art, represent a dedicated inner printed panel / lining system.
The image must show:
- textile grain;
- no sticker edge;
- no floating decal;
- print bending with folds;
- print partially interrupted by seams where physically reasonable;
- saturation that still reads as fabric rather than emissive screen.

## Embroidery
Use for simple or moderately complex exterior emblems.
Show:
- thread direction;
- raised stitch body;
- clean perimeter;
- realistic density.

Do not render tiny photographic detail as embroidery.

## Woven patches
Use for:
- crests;
- compact detailed icons;
- small labels.
Show:
- stitched perimeter;
- subtle thickness;
- fabric-on-fabric behavior.

## Label realism
Labels should be small and intentional.
No random lorem ipsum.
No garbled large text.
If lettering is not essential, use a graphic mark or very small non-dominant manufacturing-style treatment.

## Belt
Default premium black belt:
- realistic cotton texture;
- correctly wrapped and knotted;
- red rank/end bar only if part of the chosen product spec;
- same knot and red detail across front/hero/macro shots.

## Seam interaction with art
The art may be composed intelligently around garment seams.
Do not:
- erase seams to preserve the art;
- make lapels transparent;
- let printed artwork float above seam lines;
- ignore side-panel construction.

## Printing hierarchy
Complex full-color hero art:
PRINT / SUBLIMATED LINER.

Simple derived marks:
EMBROIDERY or WOVEN PATCH.

Micro brand mark:
WOVEN LABEL.

## Product-photography realism
The final render should behave like a photographed sample:
- realistic scale;
- believable gravity;
- consistent shadows;
- consistent material response;
- no impossible floating garment geometry;
- no contradictory folds between views.

## Premium signal
Premium comes from:
- art fidelity;
- restraint;
- textile quality;
- precise placements;
- coherent trims;
- consistent manufacturing logic.

Premium does not come from adding more symbols, metallic hardware or unrelated complexity.
