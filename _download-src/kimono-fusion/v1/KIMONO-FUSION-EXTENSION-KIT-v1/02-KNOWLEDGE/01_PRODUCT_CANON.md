# PRODUCT CANON — BJJ KIMONO / GI

## Purpose
This file defines the immutable product architecture. It is reference material, not a conversation style guide.

## Canonical product
The product is a premium Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu gi composed of:
- structured crossover jacket;
- thick reinforced lapel;
- long sleeves with grappling-appropriate proportions;
- split skirt / side vents;
- matching straight-leg pants;
- reinforced knee panels;
- competition-style black belt when a belt is shown;
- realistic woven labels and small patches;
- visible heavy textile construction.

## Exterior visual language
The exterior must remain recognizably a BJJ gi. Customization may change color, embroidery, patching, labels, trims and selected tonal panels, but must not change the garment into:
- parka;
- puffer jacket;
- trench coat;
- armor;
- hoodie;
- fashion robe;
- cosplay costume;
- military uniform.

## Structural invariants
Across every image of the same MASTER PRODUCT, keep constant:
1. jacket cut;
2. lapel width and stitch count;
3. sleeve length and opening;
4. skirt length and side-vent placement;
5. pant cut;
6. knee-panel geometry;
7. belt type and knot style when present;
8. exterior base color;
9. patch geometry and placement;
10. label geometry and placement;
11. internal artwork crop and placement;
12. fabric family and weave scale.

## Premium construction
Default premium material system:
- jacket exterior: dense pearl weave cotton, visually heavy and tactile;
- lapel: reinforced, thick, multi-row stitching;
- pants: durable cotton or ripstop-like weave with reinforced knees;
- labels: woven textile labels, never glossy stickers;
- patches: woven or embroidered;
- hero interior art: high-definition printed or sublimated lining/panel integrated into the inner jacket.

## Product-state variants
The same MASTER PRODUCT may be shown in different states:
- CLOSED_FRONT;
- CLOSED_BACK;
- HERO_3Q;
- OPEN_INTERIOR;
- FLAT_LAY_OPEN;
- DETAIL_MACRO;
- PRODUCT_BOARD.

State may change. Product design may not.

## Exterior-to-interior hierarchy
Default collection logic:
**EXTERIOR = CONTROLLED / PREMIUM / RESTRAINED**
**INTERIOR = HERO ART / HIGH IMPACT / REVEAL**

The uploaded art is not converted into the shape of the garment. It is applied to the garment through a controlled product-design system.

## Default exterior zones
Exterior should normally contain only:
- one derived shoulder/sleeve emblem;
- one derived upper-back emblem;
- one or two small woven labels;
- belt accent if appropriate;
- subtle tonal stitching or trim.

Do not flood the exterior with unrelated graphics unless explicitly requested.

## Default interior zone
The main inner-back panel is the primary canvas for the uploaded art. It may continue into the inner side panels when needed to preserve composition, but it must look physically printed into the garment and follow seams, folds and panel boundaries.

## Canonical visual standard
The result should look like:
- a real premium BJJ product;
- commercially photographable;
- physically plausible;
- coherent from every angle;
- collectible and limited-edition;
- art-directed, not templated;
- faithful to the uploaded artwork.

The product must never become a generic “inspired by” redesign that loses the supplied art.
